ONE LAST THING
Some people
quietly enter your life;

not with loud promises,
not with grand gestures...

Just with a simple presence,
a smile,
a look,
a short conversation.

And then one day,
you realize that
among all the noise in the world,

thinking about one person has become a kind of peace.

Maybe I don't know
exactly why.
Maybe I can't even
give this feeling a name.

But I know one thing...

Among all the people
in this world,
you are not ordinary to me. ♡
Some feelings don't need an explanation;
they only need to be real.
